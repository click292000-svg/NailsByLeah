# 2. Client Pages

Pages that let clients view salon information, explore services, and manage their bookings.

| No. | Ref | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | 2.1 | Display the salon's exact location using Google Maps, including a location pin, complete address, and a functional **"Get Directions"** link. |
| 2.2 | 2.2, 4 | Display the base price of every service in Philippine Pesos (₱), loaded from the backend price list (see backend [3. Services and Pricing](../backend/03-services-pricing.md)). |
| 2.3 | 2.4 | Replace the free-text "Note for Leah" field with predefined selectable options using a dropdown menu or selection chips, similar to Dear Doll. |
| 2.4 | 10.1 | Display available appointment time slots and visually gray out slots that are already booked or blocked. |
| 2.5 | 10.3 | Booking History page showing previous and current bookings with their statuses: **Pending, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled**. |
| 2.6 | 10.6 | Allow clients to reschedule an appointment. Disable the option (with an explanation) when the appointment is less than 24 hours away. |
| 2.7 | 2.5 | When cancelling a booking, show a dropdown with the reasons: Change of schedule, Emergency, Booked by mistake, Found another provider, and Others. |
