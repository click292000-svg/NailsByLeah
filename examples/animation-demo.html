Nail Rules

Beautiful nails, confident you.

  1. 1

    Book with intention

    Please book only if you are sure you can make it. Your slot is reserved especially for you.

  2. 2

    Deposit is required

    A non-refundable deposit of ₱150 is required to secure your appointment.

  3. 3

    Be on time

    Please arrive on time. Late arrivals may result in a shorter service or rescheduling.

  4. 4

    Cancellations

    Kindly let us know at least 24 hours in advance if you need to cancel or reschedule.

  5. 5

    Cancellation fee

    Late cancellations or no-shows will incur a fee. This protects the time reserved for you.

  6. 6

    3-day repair

    We repair any lifting or chipping. Let us know within 3 days after your appointment.Valid for repairs only.

  7. 7

    Payment policy

    Send your GCash payment right after the service. Late payments will incur a fee.

Before your appointment

  • Arrive on time so we can stay on schedule.
  • Come with clean, bare nails: no polish, gel or acrylic, unless you booked a removal.
  • No extra guests or kids: space is limited and it keeps things calm and focused.
